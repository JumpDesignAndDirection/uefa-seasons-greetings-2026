UEFA Season's Greetings 2026

Creative Proposal

Three concepts for a 45–60 second animated Season's Greetings film. Warm, festive, and hopeful — connected to football and the people who love it.

Format Animated Film · 45–60 Seconds
Delivery Email Broadcast · Digital

Jump Design & Direction
13th May 2026

Project Overview

"A warm, hopeful, and distinctly European festive message — connecting football culture with seasonal traditions."

UEFA has commissioned a new internal animated Season's Greetings film for distribution via email during the festive period. Running approximately 45–60 seconds, the film should deliver a warm, hopeful, and distinctly European message that connects football culture with seasonal tradition.

Rather than relying on archive footage, all three proposals build original visual worlds inspired by iconic UEFA moments, legendary players, and European festive iconography — achieved through stylised animation, handcrafted miniature aesthetics, and large-scale real-world spectacle.

01

Concept One

The Ball Boy

A heartfelt stop-motion-inspired journey following a tiny football-themed Christmas bauble character through a magical European Christmas market filled with UEFA-inspired festive objects, handcrafted football folklore, and toy-like environments. The story culminates beneath a giant illuminated Christmas tree where the character finds its place among a larger festive family.

Emotional Warm Handcrafted Festive Hopeful Cinematic
Visual References LAIKA Stop-Motion · European Christmas Markets · Miniature Festive Worlds
01

First Steps

The Ball Boy — a tiny football ornament with scarf and jointed limbs — takes its first tentative steps across snow-dusted cobblestones and into the magic of the market.

02

The Trophy Stall

A festive stall glows with gingerbread UEFA trophies — Champions League, Europa League, Conference League — flanked by iced shirts of the game's greatest players.

03

The Carved Legends

The Ball Boy gazes up at a carved wooden tableau commemorating the Messi Solo Run of 2011 — an iconic UEFA moment immortalised in handcrafted market sculpture.

04

Football in Miniature

Handcrafted market sculptures bring legendary UEFA moments to life — Messi threading past defenders, Ronaldo rising for the bicycle kick — each one a piece of living football folklore.

05

Snow Globe Stadiums

The Ball Boy peers into glass globes, each one a miniature UEFA stadium captured in snowfall — Wembley, Camp Nou, San Siro — tiny worlds of football history.

06

The Great Tree

Between the boots of a nearby child, the Ball Boy discovers the magnificent UEFA Christmas tree — its lights spelling out warmth and belonging against the winter sky.

07

New Companions

The child lifts the Ball Boy, so he becomes nestled amongst the tree branches, alongside a handcrafted UEFA referee ornament.

08

Season's Greetings

The grand finale: a towering festive tree blazes "Season's Greetings — UEFA" against the northern lights above a snow-covered European square, the whole village gathered beneath.

02

Concept Two

The UEFA
Gingerbread
Factory

A playful handcrafted festive bakery inspired by the symmetrical cinematic language of Wes Anderson and the tactile stop-motion charm of Fantastic Mr. Fox. Iconic football actions are transformed into simplified mechanical baking sequences that produce UEFA-themed Christmas gingerbread and European festive treats.

Playful Graphic Tactile Elegant Rhythmic European
Visual References Fantastic Mr Fox · Wes Anderson Symmetry · Stop-Motion Miniature
00

Football's Finest Festive Feasts

The sign above the factory door tells you everything you need to know. UEFA's Festive Factory has been crafting football's finest seasonal traditions since the very beginning — established 1954.

01

Boulangerie Mécanique

The factory introduces itself: "Boulangerie Mécanique / Pâtisserie de Noël." At its heart, a dough globe shaped like Europe floats above a flour-dusted workbench.

02

The Musiala Rolling Pin

Musiala dribbles past suspended defender puppets, his movement driving the rolling pin mechanism and stretching pastry dough with effortless, unstoppable precision.

03

The Ingredient Station

Foosball-style player figures kick dried fruits from overhead dispenser jars into a Champions League trophy serving as the mixing bowl — filling it with festive precision.

04

Bûche de Noël Like Beckham

A football boot rolls the perfect chocolate log on the table surface. The bakery poster behind confirms it in holly-trimmed lettering: "Bûche de Noël like Beckham."

05

The Mince Meat Cannon

Petr Čech (#1) stands ready beneath the gleaming brass Mince Meat Cannon — gloves raised, pie tin held aloft — to receive the festive filling with champion reflexes.

06

The Stollen Ceremony

Ronaldo and Van Basten perform mirrored overhead kicks into a hanging flour sieve, sending powdered sugar cascading like Christmas snowfall onto the Stollen loaf below.

07

The Tiki-Taka Machine

Wall-mounted in perfect symmetry, the roasted chestnut machine bounces chestnuts between TIKI and TAKA copper pans with metronomic rhythm, landing them neatly into paper cones.

08

The Proving Ground

The Puskás Aréna in Budapest — home of the 2026 UEFA Champions League Final — reimagined as the factory's grand proving ground. Inside, row upon row of dough letters rise steadily, gathering strength before the final bake.

09

The Icing Line

Once baked, automated icing machines pipe intricate football motifs — pitches, trophies, match balls — onto the U-E-F-A gingerbread letters as they travel the length of the decorating conveyor.

10

Season's Greetings From UEFA

The finished UEFA gingerbread letters stand proudly on a plate beside Father Christmas's fireside chair, the handwritten card behind them reading: "Season's Greetings From UEFA."

03

Concept Three

Lights
of Europe

As winter falls across Europe, Father Christmas journeys silently through the night sky as cities below awaken in light. Across stadiums, historic streets, rooftops, and landmarks, extraordinary festive football installations illuminate the continent — giant Christmas light displays inspired by iconic UEFA players, unforgettable football moments, and the unique visual language of European festive traditions.

Cinematic Epic European Nostalgic Festive Monumental
Visual References Classic Christmas Advertising · European City Light Festivals · Cinematic Aerial Photography
01

Father Christmas Over Europe

Father Christmas and his reindeer pass silently above a moonlit Europe, cities twinkling far below as the continent awakens to the festive season.

02

Ronaldo — Lisbon

In Lisbon, historic Portuguese facades transform into a giant golden light installation: Cristiano Ronaldo's iconic bicycle kick from Turin 2018, frozen in brilliant festive light.

03

Conference League Final — Leipzig

Leipzig's historic square is transformed with a towering green neon Conference League trophy installation — "CONFERENCE LEAGUE FINAL" blazing across the Gothic facades in festive light.

04

Europa League — Seville

The narrow streets of Seville glow gold with a magnificent Europa League trophy installation — "Sevilla 6 Europa" — the city's proud football identity woven into its festive light.

05

The Festive Stadium

Aerial view: a UEFA stadium wrapped in giant candy canes, oversized presents stacked on its roof, and Father Christmas passing silently overhead — a monumental Christmas spectacle from above.

06

Zlatan — Stockholm

Stockholm's winter streets are illuminated by Zlatan Ibrahimović's acrobatic volley in Swedish yellow and blue — a nation's greatest moment rendered in the language of Christmas light.

07

Istanbul 2005

Two mirrored Liverpool player figures hold the Champions League trophy aloft between them in Istanbul's historic alleyways. The message glows beneath them: "İSTANBUL 2005."

08

UEFA Women's Euro Champions 2022 — Wembley

Outside Wembley Stadium, illuminated silhouettes of celebrating England players are framed by "2022 UEFA Women's Euro Champions" — history honoured in festive light.

09

Zidane — Paris

A grand Parisian boulevard is graced by Zinedine Zidane's Champions League volley from Glasgow 2002 — the #5 shirt glowing in elegant blue and white, the Eiffel Tower behind.

10

Mbappé — Paris

Elsewhere in Paris, Kylian Mbappé's instantly recognisable crossed-arm celebration blazes across an entire building facade in bold PSG colours. "PARIS — FRANCE."

11

Neuer, The Wall — Munich

Munich's Christmas market becomes the canvas for Manuel Neuer's diving save stretching between buildings in German national colours, lit up in the winter night: "THE WALL — GERMANY."

12

Season's Greetings From UEFA

From above the clouds, Father Christmas looks down as the lights of Europe coalesce into a single glowing message — "Season's Greetings" written in light across the entire continent.
